INTRODUCTION

About Me

I am an interdisciplinary researcher working at the intersection of Consumer Psychology, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science, focusing on computational frameworks to decode and predict consumer behavior and decision-making. By combining Behavioral AI, Cognitive Analytics, and Decision Neuroscience, my research leverages Predictive Modeling, Machine Learning, Deep Learning, Natural Language Processing (NLP), and Structural Equation Modeling (SEM) to advance consumer intelligence and neuromarketing applications.

ACADEMIC FOCUS

Research Interests

My research interests lie at the intersection of Consumer Psychology, Artificial Intelligence, and Data Science, with a focus on understanding and predicting consumer decision-making through computational and data-driven approaches.

Consumer Intelligence

Using data-driven approaches to understand consumer preferences, behavior, and decision-making.

Behavioral AI

Exploring how artificial intelligence and machine learning can model and predict human behavior.

Decision Neuroscience

Understanding the cognitive and psychological process underlying human decision-making.

Neuromarketing

Investigating the cognitive and emotional factors influencing consumer responses to marketing stimuli.

Cognitive Analytics

Applying analytical and computational methods to explore cognitive processes and behavioral patterns.
